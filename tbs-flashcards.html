How to study with this deck
  1. Pick a category (left side, or the dropdown on a phone).
  2. Read the front and answer it out loud before you flip.
  3. Flip the card, then mark it Got it or Still learning. Your progress saves on this device.
  4. Switch the filter to Still learning to drill only the cards you missed.
  5. Use Quiz to practice multiple choice like the test. Misses go to Still learning automatically.
  6. Turn on Answer first to practice the other direction.
  7. See a mistake? Hit Edit on the card. Every change is kept in its history.
